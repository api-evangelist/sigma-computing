To generate a HAR file in Edge: Press F12 or Ctrl Shift + I Navigate to the Network tab at the top of DevTools Ensure “Preserve Log” and “Disable Cache” are checked. Click start recording in the top left hand corner of the dev tools Reproduce the issue, keeping DevTools Open Click the download button on the right side of the top tool bar to export the HAR file
