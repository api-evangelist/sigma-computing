If you manage multiple customers, business units, or regions with Sigma Tenants, you don’t want to rebuild the same workbook in every tenant. Deployment lets you build once in a parent organization, then push workbooks, reports, and data models to as many tenants as you need. When you publish a change, every tenant gets it.
